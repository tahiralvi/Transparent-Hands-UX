How to donate using UBL Netbanking? (For Netbanking users only)

UBL Netbanking provides you an easy and fast service to make donations to a list of charity funds. Follow the simple steps below to make donations.

  • Log on to UBL Netbanking
  • Go to 'Pay Bills & Prepaid'. There is a list of charity organizations and donation funds under section
  • 'Donations / Zakat'
  • Click on the 'Pay' button of the selected account
  • Enter amount (PKR) and comments
  • Enter Contribution ID

Donate via Telenor Easy Paisa

Customers can send donations at anytime by visiting any of the Easypaisa shops across the country. A message from 3737 on your mobile phone gives you a confirmation for the successful transfer of your donation amount to the organization.

  • Customer will visit any Easypaisa outlet & asks the merchant to send donation
  • Merchant will ask the customer for the following:
    • Organization to whom donation has to be made
      • Transparent Hands
    • Donation Amount
    • Customer Mobile number
  • Easypaisa merchant will complete the transaction and the customer will pay the merchant the donation amount.
  • Both the Merchant and the Customer will receive a confirmation sms from 3737.
